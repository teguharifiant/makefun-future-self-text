# Texts From Your Future Self

Entry for the [Texts From Your Future Self](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun/): one funny text message, under
200 characters, from the writer's future self — funny by itself, with no
setup and no context, just the message as it would arrive.

## The entry

> Future you here. The password you just changed to something
> ‘unforgettable’? I’ve been locked out since 2031. The hint is our first
> pet. We never had a pet.

156 characters. The joke: the future self is not warning about anything
grand — he has been locked out of his own account for years because of a
password the present self just set, and the recovery hint asks about a pet
that never existed. Specific, mundane, and the twist lands in the last
sentence.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the text above to `/output/entry.txt` (Node 22+, no
dependencies). Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
